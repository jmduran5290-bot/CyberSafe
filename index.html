STAY SAFE ONLINE

Cybersecurity Safety Tips

Follow these simple habits to protect your information and online accounts.

🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for your accounts. Avoid reusing the same password.

🔗

Check Links First

Inspect unfamiliar links before opening them. Be careful with unexpected messages.

🔒

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Use an additional verification method to help protect your accounts.

📱

Keep Devices Updated

Install updates from trusted sources to receive security improvements.

🙅

Never Share OTPs

Do not share one-time passwords or verification codes with other people.

📢

Think Before You Share

Avoid posting sensitive personal information publicly online.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

About CyberSafe

A cybersecurity awareness website designed to help students understand online risks and develop safer digital habits.

🛡️

Stay Smart. Stay Safe.

CyberSafe aims to provide students with accessible cybersecurity information through lessons, quizzes, interactive games, and practical scenarios.

Our Objectives

  • Increase awareness of common online threats.
  • Teach students how to recognize suspicious messages.
  • Encourage strong password practices.
  • Promote responsible and safe internet use.
